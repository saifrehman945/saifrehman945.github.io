abstract: >
  Developed and validated a 2D CFD model in ANSYS Fluent to study the combined impact of Al2O3 nanofluids
  (varying ethylene-glycol/water ratios) and artificial roughness on solar-heater performance. Examines how rib
  geometry, fluid composition, and turbulence interact to influence heat transfer, pressure drop, and
  thermal-hydraulic efficiency across Re = 3,800-18,000.

**Highlights:** triangular ribs → up to **71.6%** heat-transfer enhancement; **40:60 EG/water** best thermal-hydraulic balance (peak PEC ≈ 1.5); higher EG improves thermal performance while reducing friction penalty; validated to <8% error.
