abstract: >
  Investigated unsteady ventilated super-cavitation around underwater projectiles using high-fidelity CFD combined
  with data-driven modeling. Unsteady RANS with a VOF multiphase formulation resolves cavity dynamics, pressure
  evolution, and drag characteristics for disk- and cone-based cavitators, validated against experimental and
  numerical benchmarks. A Kolmogorov-Arnold Network (KAN) surrogate then predicts drag from CFD time-series,
  coupling physics-based simulation with fast reduced-order models for real-time evaluation of cavitating flows.
